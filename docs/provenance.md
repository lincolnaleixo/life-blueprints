# Provenance and Evidence

The initial collection combines research synthesis, public platform documentation, public operator reports, and lessons generalized from private implementations.

Private material is never copied here. A lesson crosses the public boundary only after it is rewritten as a generic claim with private names, paths, agreements, and identifying evidence removed.

Evidence strength should remain explicit:

- platform documentation supports current mechanics and policies;
- datasets and audited reports support stronger quantitative claims;
- marketplace listings and creator reports are directional and may contain selection bias;
- one implementation can reveal a useful pattern but cannot prove universal applicability;
- unknown or unstable claims should say `verify` rather than present false certainty.

When a claim changes materially, prefer linking the primary public source near the claim. Avoid decorative citations that do not directly support it.
