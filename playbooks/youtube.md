# YouTube Playbook

Options to help a channel reach its next [Action Map trigger](../action-maps/youtube.md). This is a menu of experiments to try, not a checklist, capability catalog or promise of growth. Techniques are available at any stage.

## Choose one

Look at the current video, available aggregate evidence and production friction. Pick the clearest bottleneck, then choose one small task with a plausible benefit and manageable effort. If evidence is missing, say so and treat the choice as a hypothesis.

| What needs attention | One possible task | What to look at afterward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No representative video yet | Pick one audience question and make one AI-assisted, human-reviewed video answering it. | A public video that delivers its stated promise. |
| The topic or promise is unclear | Narrow the next video to one specific question the intended audience cares about. | Whether viewers can identify its subject and expected value. |
| People see the video but rarely click | Test one clearer title **or** thumbnail, faithfully representing the content. | Impressions and click-through rate, when enough comparable evidence exists. |
| Viewers leave before the useful part | Rewrite the opening to deliver on the promise sooner; remove one unnecessary section. | Aggregate retention, when available, and whether the opening delivers its promise. |
| Production repeatedly needs manual help | Turn the most repetitive step into reusable code, keeping a human check and safe recovery. | Whether the next execution needs less intervention without reducing quality. |
| Audio or visuals obscure the message | Fix one concrete issue in the next video, such as unclear narration or unreadable text. | A direct before/after quality check. |

## Turn the choice into work

Use one short sentence in the channel's plan:

> Change **[one thing]** because **[observed bottleneck or hypothesis]**; check **[evidence]** at **[next review]**.

The assistant helps choose and explain that one task; it does not copy this menu into the plan. Diagnostic metrics help choose a technique but do not replace the Action Map's views trigger. Small samples remain uncertain; a change in views alone does not prove which technique caused it. Do not publish another video early just to try a technique if the channel's current plan reserves it for the next stage. Preserve already-approved, healthy production.

Retain rights, safety and publication-approval boundaries. Add techniques only when real work gives a reason to keep them; no future-stage catalog is needed now.
